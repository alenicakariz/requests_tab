Process Status Flow

 1  For Clearance - The requester should settle the deficiency/ies to necessary office/s. Click "View Details" to view current Clearance's Status

 2  For Receiving - The request is currently in Receiving Window and waiting for the client's submission of requirements

 3  For Evaluation/Processing - Evaluation and Processing of records and required documents for releasing.

 4  Ready for Pickup - The requested document/s is/are already available for pickup at the releasing section of student records.

 5   Released - The requested document/s was/were claimed.

ADVISORY TO OUR CLIENTS ▼

As part of our monitoring and evaluation, and to improve the quality of our system and services, all requests in the Online Document Request System (ODRS) with incomplete and invalid requirements shall be automatically deleted after 90 days of noncompliance. Please be advised that the deletion from the system may be done without prior notice or approval from the requesting client.

Please be guided accordingly.

Payment Options

Choose a payment option from the following:

  • PUP Cashier
  • 1 Print the Payment Voucher after the submission of request.
  • 2 Pay at the Cashier's Office (Main Building, Ground Floor South Wing)
  • 3 Go to the Student Records Office for the Processing of Request/s (Main Building, Ground Floor South Wing)

OR

  • Link.BizPortal Payment
  • 1 Login to your Account and go to the Requests List Page
  • 2 Click the Pay Online (Link.BizPortal) button.
  • 3 The payment will reflect to the system within the day
  • 4 For transaction made thru Pesonet and Cash Options, Payment will be posted 3 to 5 banking days
